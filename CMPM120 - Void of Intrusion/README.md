### Controls
A/D or LeftArrow/RightArrow - Move Left/Right
Spacebar - Shoot

### Score
Points/Score is tracked through the amount of player bullets that hit an enemy 
(no bonus points on defeat)

### Bonus Elements
Multiple Levels - Enemies will be given increased speed in higher levels, and movement patterns between the 3 waves will vary across 6 levels. Upon reaching further than level 6, the game will repeat the patterns starting from level 1.
